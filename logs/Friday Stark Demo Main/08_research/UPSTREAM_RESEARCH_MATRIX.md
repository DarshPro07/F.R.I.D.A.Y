## Research conclusions
- Runtime equality is the wrong goal; integration rigor is equal, routing is task-specific.
- Code-intelligence tools are prioritized early because they can reduce the cost of integrating everything else.
- OpenHands/Cline/CrewAI/agenticSeek overlap orchestration. They must not own Friday's parent objective.
- Maxun/OpenMontage/Postiz and agenticSeek require explicit copyleft-aware boundaries.
- Open Notebook/AnythingLLM/GBrain overlap; each needs a distinct information role.
- Pipecat must compete against the already-working LiveKit pipeline rather than replace it by assumption.
